## Full Description

**Know what's really in your products.**

WonderFat Scanner lets you instantly analyze any food or skincare product by scanning its barcode. Get a clear health score from 0-100, see detailed ingredient breakdowns, and make smarter choices for you and your family.

**How It Works:**
1. Point your camera at any product barcode
2. Get an instant health score (0-100)
3. See a full breakdown of ingredients, nutrition, and additives
4. Discover healthier alternatives

**Key Features:**
- Barcode scanner for food and cosmetic products
- Health score based on nutrition, additives, and ingredient quality
- Color-coded ratings: Excellent, Good, Mediocre, or Poor
- Detailed ingredient analysis with risk levels
- Nutritional breakdown with visual charts
- Scan history and favorites
- Personalized recommendations based on your dietary preferences and skin type
- Share product scores with friends and family

**Scoring System:**
- Food products: 60% nutritional quality + 30% additive safety + 10% organic certification
- Skincare products: 100% ingredient safety analysis against known harmful chemicals

**Clean Living Made Simple:**
WonderFat believes in pure, natural ingredients — in our products and in yours. Our app helps you navigate the overwhelming world of product labels so you can choose what's truly good for your body.

**From the makers of WonderFat Whipped Tallow Balm** — a skincare product with just 5 clean, natural ingredients. No synthetics, no fillers, no compromises.
